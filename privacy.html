What this website collects

This is a static page: it sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and makes no requests to any third-party service. There is no form on this site, and no data is collected simply by visiting it. The typeface is self-hosted rather than loaded from a font service for the same reason — so that visiting this page never sends a request anywhere but here.

Server logs

Our hosting provider may automatically record technical information about visits — such as IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, and request timestamps — in standard server logs, for security and operational purposes such as detecting abuse and keeping the site running. We do not use these logs for analytics or profiling. We will name our hosting provider here once it is finalised.

Email correspondence

If you email us, we process the contents of your message and your email address in order to respond to you. Our lawful basis for this is legitimate interests — specifically, our interest in answering enquiries sent to us. We keep correspondence for as long as is reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry and maintain a record of the relationship, after which it is deleted.

Your rights under UK GDPR

If we hold personal data about you (for example, because you have emailed us), you have the right to:

  • request that we correct inaccurate data;

  • restrict or object to how we process your data; and

To exercise any of these rights, email info@ardwickpartners.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK regulator for data protection, at ico.org.uk or by calling their helpline on 0303 123 1113.
